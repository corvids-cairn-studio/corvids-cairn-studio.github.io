A design detour into why relationships needed to be load-bearing before combat did.

Project Last Light isn't a combat game that happens to have a story — the story is the point, and combat exists to support it. So before I locked down move sets or damage numbers, I needed the bond system working first, since it's what most of the narrative actually hangs off of.

More on how that system is shaping up soon.
